Welcome to *ICANN DNS Engineering website*  
![ICANN Logo](/media/uploads/2012/03/logo-greyscale-94x94.png "icann-logo-94x94.png")

ICANN DNS Engineering Team is responsible for

- All the operations of the [ICANN Managed Root Server](/imrs) (**IMRS**), part of the [Root Name Server System](http://root-servers.org)
- ICANN DNS domain name portfolio
- DNSSEC infrastructure for ICANN Managed domains
- Operations of ICANN [authoritative](/services/authoritative-dns/) DNS Servers
- The Operations for the IANA reverse domains **in-addr.arpa** and **ip6.arpa** (among others)
- As well many [other services](/services/)

Team [members](/aboutus/) of ICANN DNS Engineering are regularly attending technical meetings such as [NANOG](http://nanog.org/meetings), [AUSNOG](http://www.ausnog.net/events), [NZNOG](http://www.nznog.org), [SANOG](http://www.sanog.org), [LACNOG](http://www.lacnog.org), etc. to talk about various aspects regarding on-going projects. The team also participates in the [IETF](https://www.ietf.org/meeting/) and [DNS-OARC](https://www.dns-oarc.net) forums.
