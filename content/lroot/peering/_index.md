Thank you for your expressed interest in peering with an **L-Root** (L-Root) instance.  
Currently the L-Root peers with the Autonomous System Number **AS20144**.

In most of our exchange points we ONLY peer with route servers.  
Complete peering information can be found here: <http://as20144.peeringdb.com>.

**Has your organization considered hosting its own L-Root instance?**

By hosting an L-Root instance (L-Single) you help provide your Internet users with a more resilient Internet, reducing the response time your users experience during DNS queries, and at the same time, demonstrate your organization’s commitment to a resilient and stable Internet. For additional information, visit [https://dns.icann.org/lroot/host/](https://dns.icann.org/lroot/host/).

If you are willing to consider hosting an L-Root instance, please contact your nearest [ICANN Global Stakeholder Engagement representative](https://www.icann.org/en/about/staff/gse), who can facilitate your organization having its own L-Root instance.

For more information about hosting your own L-Single, please refer to our [L-Single F.A.Q.](/lroot/faq/)
